Distributed Word Representations: A Comparative Analysis of Skip-gram and GloVe
This repository contains the bachelor thesis of Georg Mykjåland Risøy, titled "Distributed Word Representations: A Comparative Analysis of Skip-gram and GloVe", submitted on 1st May 2025 as part of the Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Groningen.

Abstract
This thesis provides a comparative analysis of two classical word embedding models in natural language processing: Skip-gram and GloVe. It explores their representations of semantic meaning, training objectives, and underlying assumptions, and evaluates their performance using standard analogy tasks.

The work further discusses how these models laid the foundation for modern approaches in NLP, such as transformer-based systems, and reflects on the practical and theoretical significance of distributed representations.

Key Contributions
In-depth comparison between Skip-gram and GloVe:

Local context prediction vs. global co-occurrence

Training efficiency and interpretability

Evaluation on word analogy tasks

Analysis of semantic geometry in vector spaces

Contextual insights into the legacy of static embeddings in modern NLP models
